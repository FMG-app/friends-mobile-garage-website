Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

IVIGO ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, shared, and protected when you use our website, customer marketplace and grocery applications, and related services.

This policy should be read together with any applicable terms presented in the IVIGO app. Please contact us if you have questions about how your information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, mobile number, email address, account identifiers, and profile details.
  • Authentication information: information needed to verify your phone number or sign you in, including verification-related metadata from authentication providers. We do not ask you to disclose an OTP to our support staff.
  • Delivery information: delivery address, city, postal code, and location data when you grant location access or choose location-based features.
  • Shopping information: cart contents, orders, product preferences, ratings, returns, exchanges, and customer-support requests.
  • Payment information: transaction IDs, payment status, payment method references, and refund information needed to process or reconcile transactions.
  • Technical information: app version, device and diagnostic information, security signals, and logs used to operate and protect the service.
  • Merchant or delivery information: relevant business, identity-verification, shop, order-fulfilment, and delivery details if you use those roles.

2. How We Use Information

  • Register, authenticate, and manage accounts, including phone verification and Google Sign-In where offered.
  • Display products, manage carts, process orders, and coordinate fulfilment and delivery.
  • Use delivery addresses and, when permitted, device location to support relevant delivery and location features.
  • Process payments, refunds, returns, exchanges, and customer inquiries.
  • Send transactional messages such as verification codes, order updates, and service notifications.
  • Protect accounts, detect suspicious activity, troubleshoot errors, and improve service reliability.
  • Meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations where applicable.

3. Phone Authentication and Google Sign-In

Where enabled, IVIGO uses Firebase Authentication for phone number verification and account sign-in. Google and its service providers may process phone numbers, app or device verification signals, and related technical information to authenticate users and prevent abuse, subject to their applicable policies. SMS delivery depends on network and provider conditions.

If you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information made available through that sign-in process, such as your name, email address, and account identifier, according to the permissions and configuration used.

4. Location and Delivery Addresses

IVIGO may request device location permission to help identify a delivery area, support location-based shopping, or assist with delivery-related features. You can manage location permissions in your device settings. You may also be able to enter a delivery address manually. Location data is used for relevant service functions and is not intended to be collected without an applicable permission or other lawful basis.

5. Payments and Refunds

Online payments may be handled by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay where supported. Payment providers process sensitive payment credentials under their own policies. IVIGO may retain payment references, transaction status, and refund records needed to fulfil orders, provide support, and maintain records. Cash on delivery may be offered where available.

6. Information Sharing

We may share only information reasonably needed to operate the services with relevant merchants, delivery personnel, logistics providers, payment processors, authentication and cloud providers, customer-support providers, and competent authorities where required by law. For example, delivery partners may need a recipient's name, address, and contact number to complete a delivery.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

Our services may rely on providers such as Google Firebase (authentication, databases, storage, and messaging), Google Sign-In, payment processors, and delivery or logistics providers. Their processing may be subject to their own privacy notices and terms. Features and providers may vary by app and service.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure. Do not share verification codes or passwords with anyone.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, fulfil orders, handle disputes, maintain required records, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of data and applicable obligations.

10. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. If account-deletion functionality is available in your IVIGO app, you can submit a request through the account settings. You can also contact us by email for privacy or account-deletion assistance.

Removing an account does not necessarily require immediate deletion of records that must be retained for transactions, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

11. Notifications, Cookies, and Diagnostics

We may use push notifications to provide service updates where enabled. You can control notification permissions in your device settings. Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for necessary functionality, security, and, where implemented, analytics.

12. Children's Privacy

IVIGO services are not intended for children to use independently where parental or guardian authorization is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The updated version will be published here with a revised effective date.
