Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Friends Mobile Garage respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website, mobile application, marketplace and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Delivery and billing information.
  • Account and login-related information.
  • Order, product and transaction information.
  • Payment-related references required to process orders.
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support.
  • Technical information required for website and application operation.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage customer or merchant accounts.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Arrange product delivery and related services.
  • Process payments and refunds through applicable payment providers.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our products, services and user experience.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Payments

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. Friends Mobile Garage does not intend to store your complete payment card, banking or other sensitive payment credentials on its own systems when those credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider.

Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information when necessary to provide our services, including with payment providers, delivery or logistics partners, merchants, technology/service providers, customer-support providers, or authorities where legally required.

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or related services may use cookies or similar technologies where required for functionality, security, analytics or user experience. Browser settings may allow you to control certain cookies.

7. Account Information

If you create an account, you are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for providing accurate information.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Choices

Subject to applicable law and operational requirements, you may contact us to request access, correction or other appropriate action regarding your personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services

Our website, application or marketplace may use third-party services for payments, authentication, hosting, delivery, analytics or other functions. Those providers may have their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
