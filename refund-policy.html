Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This policy explains the general cancellation, return, exchange and refund process for purchases made through Friends Mobile Garage.

Return, exchange and refund eligibility may depend on the product, merchant policy, order status and the applicable terms shown at the time of purchase.

1. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before the order reaches a stage where cancellation is no longer operationally possible.

Once an order has been processed, packed, shipped or otherwise handed over for fulfillment, cancellation may no longer be available.

2. Return Window

Where a product is eligible for return, customers may generally submit a return request within the applicable return period shown for the order or product.

For eligible delivered products, the platform may provide a return request window of up to 7 days from delivery, subject to the product-specific conditions and applicable policy.

3. Eligible Return Reasons

Depending on the product and applicable policy, a return or refund request may be considered for reasons such as:

  • Product received damaged.
  • Product received defective or not functioning as expected.
  • Wrong product received.
  • Product materially different from the listing.
  • Other reasons specifically permitted by the applicable product policy.

4. Non-Returnable or Restricted Products

Certain products may be non-returnable or subject to special conditions because of their nature, hygiene requirements, activation status, usage, damage caused after delivery, or merchant/manufacturer policy.

5. Product Condition for Return

Customers may be required to return the product in an acceptable condition with applicable accessories, packaging, manuals, invoices and other items supplied with the order.

A return request may be rejected where the product has been materially damaged, misused, altered or is missing required components, subject to applicable consumer law.

6. Exchange

Where exchange is available, customers may request an exchange for an eligible product within the applicable return/exchange period.

Exchange availability depends on product eligibility and replacement stock. If a replacement is unavailable, an eligible refund may be offered according to the applicable policy.

7. Return Verification

Return requests may be reviewed before approval. The platform or applicable merchant may verify the reason, product condition, supporting information and returned item before a refund or exchange is finalized.

8. Refund Processing

Once an eligible refund is approved and processed, the refund may be initiated through the applicable payment method or payment provider.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account can depend on the payment provider, bank and payment method.

9. Online Payments

For orders paid online, approved refunds may be processed through the applicable payment provider and may be returned to the eligible source payment method, subject to provider rules.

10. Cash on Delivery Orders

For COD orders, the refund method may depend on the information and payment details required to process the approved refund. Additional verification may be requested where necessary.

11. Refund Amount

The refund amount will be determined based on the approved return or cancellation request and the applicable order and product policy. Applicable non-refundable charges, if any, will be communicated where relevant.

12. Refunds and Merchant Payouts

Where an order is eligible for a refund, the refund process takes priority over merchant settlement for the affected transaction. A merchant payout may be withheld while a refund, return or exchange is pending.

13. Failed or Delayed Refunds

If an approved refund has been initiated but is not reflected within the expected processing period, customers should contact customer support with the order and refund details so the transaction can be checked.

14. How to Request a Return or Refund

Customers can use the return/exchange functionality available in the Friends Mobile Garage application for eligible orders. The request should include accurate information and any requested supporting details.

15. Fraudulent or Misuse Claims

Repeated, fraudulent, misleading or abusive return and refund requests may be reviewed and may result in restrictions on account or marketplace functionality, subject to applicable law.

Important: This page describes the general platform policy. Product-specific return, exchange and refund conditions shown during purchase may also apply.

16. Policy Changes

Friends Mobile Garage may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
