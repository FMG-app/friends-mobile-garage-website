Shipping Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Shipping Policy explains the general delivery process for products purchased through Friends Mobile Garage.

Delivery availability and estimated delivery times may vary depending on the product, merchant, destination, logistics partner and other operational conditions.

1. Shipping Availability

Products may be delivered to serviceable locations supported by Friends Mobile Garage and its logistics partners. Availability is determined during the ordering process.

2. Order Processing

After an order is placed and successfully confirmed, the applicable merchant may prepare the product for dispatch. Processing time can vary depending on product availability, merchant operations and order details.

3. Delivery Time

Estimated delivery dates or time ranges shown during checkout are estimates and are not guaranteed. Actual delivery may vary because of location, logistics conditions, holidays, weather, operational delays or circumstances beyond reasonable control.

4. Shipping Charges

Applicable delivery or shipping charges, if any, may be displayed during checkout before the order is placed.

5. Tracking

Where tracking is available, customers may receive shipment or tracking information through the Friends Mobile Garage application or other applicable communication channels.

6. Delivery Attempts

Delivery may require the customer or an authorized recipient to be available at the delivery address. The logistics partner may make reasonable delivery attempts according to its operational process.

7. Incorrect Address or Contact Details

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery address and contact information. Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delivery delays, failed delivery or additional operational requirements.

8. Damaged Package

If a package appears damaged at the time of delivery, customers should report the issue as soon as reasonably possible and provide photographs or other supporting information when requested.

9. Wrong or Missing Product

If a customer receives a wrong product or discovers missing items, the issue should be reported through the applicable return, exchange or customer-support process as soon as possible.

10. Delivery Delays

Delays may occur due to high order volumes, logistics disruptions, weather, public holidays, transportation issues, service-area limitations or other circumstances outside reasonable control.

Important: A delivery estimate is not a guarantee of delivery on a particular date. Customers should refer to the order information in the application for the latest available status.

11. Undelivered or Returned Shipments

If a shipment cannot be delivered after applicable delivery attempts, it may be returned to the merchant or designated fulfillment location. Further action may depend on the reason for non-delivery and the applicable order policy.

12. Cash on Delivery

COD availability may depend on the product, destination and operational conditions. Where COD is selected, payment is collected according to the delivery process applicable to the order.

13. Multiple Products or Merchants

Orders containing products from different merchants or fulfillment locations may be delivered in separate shipments and may have different delivery estimates.

14. Contact and Support

If you have a delivery-related concern, please contact Friends Mobile Garage with your order details so that the applicable order and shipment status can be reviewed.

15. Policy Changes

Friends Mobile Garage may update this Shipping Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
